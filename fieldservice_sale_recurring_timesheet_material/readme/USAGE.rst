#. Sell a product configured with *Field Service Tracking* set to *Create a
   recurring order* and confirm the sale order.
#. Open the recurring order with the **FSM Recurring Orders** smart button and
   click **Start**: the field service orders are generated from the frequency
   rules.
#. On a generated field service order, record the time spent in the
   **Timesheets** tab and the materials consumed on site, then click **Add to
   Sale Order**. To do it for all the orders of the recurring order at once,
   click **Add to Sale Order** on the recurring order instead.
#. The lines are added to the sale order that sold the recurring order, where
   they can be invoiced as usual. Each invoice line is linked to the field
   service order it bills.

Clicking **Add to Sale Order** again, on an order or on the recurring order,
only adds the timesheets and materials that have not been billed yet.
