This module adds a load sheet to field service day routes, so the person
doing the route knows what to load in the van in the morning and what to
deliver to each customer, following the order of the route.

The quantities come from the pending stock moves linked to the orders of the
day route (as done by `fieldservice_sale_stock` when confirming a sale order).
Deliveries are the moves to the customer and pick-ups the moves from the
customer, such as the ones generated by sale order lines with negative
quantities, whatever the operation type of their transfer. Pick-ups are only
displayed per stop: they are never counted in the products to load. Moves
already done or cancelled are left out, except for completed orders, which
show what was delivered and picked up and are displayed in green.

The day route form gets two new tabs:

- **Product Totals**: the quantity to deliver of each product, in the product
  unit of measure, with its weight and the total weight to load. Products
  without enough stock reserved are displayed in red.
- **Delivery Breakdown**: one card per order, in route order, with the
  location, a link to the order, its address (opening a map), phone numbers,
  notes, the products to deliver and the ones to pick up (in yellow).
  Deliveries without enough stock reserved are displayed in red.

Both tabs are adapted to small screens and are kept up to date when orders
are added to, removed from or changed in the day route. A printable PDF
version of the load sheet is also available.
