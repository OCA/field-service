1. Go to *Field Service > Operations > Day Routes* and open a day route.
2. Open the **Product Totals** tab to see what has to be loaded and its total
   weight. Lines in red do not have enough stock reserved.
3. Open the **Delivery Breakdown** tab to see the stops of the route. Click on
   a card title or on the order reference to open the order, on the address to
   open it on a map and on a phone number to call the customer. Deliveries in
   red do not have enough stock reserved.
4. Click on **Print Load Sheet** (or *Print > Load Sheet*) to get the PDF.

The notes of a stop are the ones of the order or, if it has none, the ones of
its location. Cancelled orders are left out.
