This module adds a load sheet to field service day routes, so the person
doing the route knows what to load in the van in the morning and what to
deliver to each customer, following the order of the route.

The quantities come from the pending stock moves linked to the orders of
the day route (as done by ``fieldservice_sale_stock`` when confirming a
sale order). Deliveries are the moves to the customer and pick-ups the
moves from the customer, such as the ones generated by sale order lines
with negative quantities, whatever the operation type of their transfer.
Pick-ups are only displayed per stop: they are never counted in the
products to load. Moves already done or cancelled are left out, except
for completed orders, which show what was delivered and picked up and
are displayed in green.

The day route form gets two new tabs:

- **Product Totals**: the quantity to deliver of each product, in the
  product unit of measure, with its weight and the total weight to load.
  Products without enough stock reserved are displayed in red.
- **Delivery Breakdown**: one card per order, in route order, with the
  location, a link to the order, its address (opening a map), phone
  numbers, notes, the products to deliver and the ones to pick up (in
  yellow). Deliveries without enough stock reserved are displayed in
  red.

Both tabs are adapted to small screens and are kept up to date when
orders are added to, removed from or changed in the day route. A
printable PDF version of the load sheet is also available.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Field Service > Operations > Day Routes* and open a day route.
2. Open the **Product Totals** tab to see what has to be loaded and its
   total weight. Lines in red do not have enough stock reserved.
3. Open the **Delivery Breakdown** tab to see the stops of the route.
   Click on a card title or on the order reference to open the order, on
   the address to open it on a map and on a phone number to call the
   customer. Deliveries in red do not have enough stock reserved.
4. Click on **Print Load Sheet** (or *Print > Load Sheet*) to get the
   PDF.

The notes of a stop are the ones of the order or, if it has none, the
ones of its location. Cancelled orders are left out.
