Calibration certificates are managed by Field Service managers. Field users
can read them but cannot create, edit or delete them.

1. As a Field Service manager, open an equipment (*Field Service > Master Data >
   Equipments*).
2. On the **Calibration** tab, add a certificate with its validity dates and
   attach the certificate file.
3. Follow expirations from *Field Service > Master Data > Calibration
   Certificates* (filter or group by expiration date).

The certificate company follows the company of its equipment, also when the
equipment company changes later.
