Install the module: the equipment portal page gains a "Service Orders" section listing the service orders of the equipment (latest first).

The orders are read with the rights of the visitor and only those of the equipment company are listed. Portal users therefore need `fieldservice_portal`, which grants them their own orders; only orders in stages marked *Visible in Portal* are shown then. Visitors who open the page through its access token (QR code) do not see the order history.
