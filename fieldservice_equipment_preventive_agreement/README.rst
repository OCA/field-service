Preventive visits driven by agreements.

Each agreement lists the preventive visit types its equipments get, with
their interval; the visits of an equipment follow the agreement it
belongs to. Visit types the agreement does not list are not planned for
its equipments.

A preventive visit can be restricted to the equipments of one agreement,
which is also set on the orders it generates. Only the agreements of the
equipments of the location can be chosen, and when there is a single one
it is preselected. The agreement can be made mandatory per company in
the Field Service settings.

Usage
=====

On an agreement, tab *Preventive Visits*: add one rule per visit type
with its interval and optional template. The equipments of the agreement
get these visits; visits added by hand on an equipment are kept.

On a preventive recurring order, *Preventive Agreement* restricts the
visits to the equipments of that agreement, which is also set on the
generated orders. Only agreements with equipments at the location are
offered; a single one is preselected. *Settings > Field Service >
Require Agreement on Preventive Visits* makes the field mandatory for
the current company.
