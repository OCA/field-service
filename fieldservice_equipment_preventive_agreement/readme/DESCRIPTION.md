Preventive visits driven by agreements.

Each agreement lists the preventive visit types its equipments get, with their
interval; the visits of an equipment follow the agreement it belongs to. Visit
types the agreement does not list are not planned for its equipments.

A preventive visit can be restricted to the equipments of one agreement, which
is also set on the orders it generates. Only the agreements of the equipments
of the location can be chosen, and when there is a single one it is
preselected. The agreement can be made mandatory per company in the Field
Service settings.
