Print 60x40mm QR code labels for field service equipments. The QR code
points to the equipment's portal page (`fieldservice_equipment_portal`), so
anyone scanning the label on the physical asset lands on its page — with a
portal access token the page can even be shared with people outside Odoo.

The label shows the company (or a dedicated "labels logo" configurable on
the company form), the equipment name and its serial number.
