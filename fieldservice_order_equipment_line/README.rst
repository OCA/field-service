Adds an equipment checklist to field service orders: one line per
equipment, ordered by sequence, that the worker marks as done or not
found while on site.

The equipments of the checklist are kept in the *Equipments* of the
order.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

On a service order, tab *Equipments*, section *Checklist*: add one line
per equipment to check and mark each line as *Done* or *Not Found* while
on site.

The checklist drives the *Equipments* of the order: an equipment is
added with its first line and removed with its last one. Equipments
added directly to the order are not turned into checklist lines.
