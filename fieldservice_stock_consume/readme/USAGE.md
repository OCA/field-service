On a service order, *Consume from Stock* opens a wizard: choose a warehouse of
the company of the order and the storable parts with their quantities, then
*Create Delivery*. A delivery in waiting state is created for the warehouse
team, linked to the order through its procurement group and addressed to the
inventory location of the location of the order. The wizard then closes back
to the order, where the parts are listed in the *Operations* tab.
