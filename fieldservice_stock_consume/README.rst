Let field technicians request parts from stock on a field service order,
without going through a sale.

A **Consume from Stock** button on the FSM order opens a wizard where
the technician picks the warehouse and the parts. Confirming creates a
waiting delivery linked to the order (through its procurement group),
targeted at the order location's inventory location: the warehouse team
then reserves and validates the delivery.

The stock documents are created with elevated rights on purpose:
technicians are usually not inventory users — they request, the
warehouse delivers.
