Preventive visits for field service equipments.

Preventive visit types (preventive maintenance, calibration, certification...)
are configurable. Each equipment lists the visit types it needs, with their
interval and the dates of the last and next visit.

A recurring order flagged as *Preventive Visit* does not carry a fixed list of
equipments: every order it generates is filled with a checklist of what is due
at the location before the following visit, most overdue first, one line per
equipment and visit type. Marking a line as done registers the visit on the
equipment; lines left pending when the order is completed are carried over to
the next visit.
