To use this module, you need to:

1. Go to **Field Service > Master Data > Locations**.
2. Provide full address information and geolocalize the location.
   Demo locations are already placed around Bangkok, so the sample orders
   show on the map after install.
3. Go to **Field Service > Dashboard**.
4. Open the map view. A colored pin is the service location (blue New,
   green Completed, grey Cancelled) and a person icon is the assigned
   worker. The same icons are drawn on the Google roadmap and on the
   OpenStreetMap base map (map type switcher). Locations and workers
   also have their own map under Field Service > Master Data.
