Open an equipment that has a product and a serial number and click
*Transfer Stock*: choose the target stock location and confirm. An internal
transfer of the serial is created and validated, and the field service
location of the equipment is updated when the target stock location is linked
to one. The *Location History* tab lists the transfers with their pickings.

The transfer uses the internal operation type of the source location's
company (preferring its warehouse) and always moves the equipment's own
serial: it is refused when that serial is not available in the source
location or is reserved by another operation. Field service users without
inventory rights can transfer the equipments they can access.
